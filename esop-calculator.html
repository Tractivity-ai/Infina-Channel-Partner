Important Notes

Fair Market Value (FMV)

The average of the stock's price at the start and end of the date of exercise.

Infina Funding

Infina funds up to 50% of the CMP. Any amount above this limit and payable to the employer becomes the employee's contribution, payable via cash or by pledging additional securities.

Exercise Price

The locked-in price at which you get to buy your shares. Set when your ESOPs were granted, usually much lower than today's FMV.

Employee Contribution

The amount you arrange after Infina funds 50% of your share's market value. It covers your exercise cost, perquisite tax, and applicable charges.
